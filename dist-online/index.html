          About the game

          Play Roadster Dash online

          Drive a Tesla Roadster through traffic in this free 3D endless runner. Change lanes, jump over cars, fly with thrusters and land barrel rolls. Manage your air supply, collect power-ups and challenge the global and regional leaderboards.

          How to play: PC and mobile controls, air and scoring tips

          Is this Tesla Surfers?

          The game is called Roadster Dash. Fans of Subway Surfers can try a car-based endless runner with lane dodging, jumps, flying and aerial tricks. This is an independent fan game, not an official Tesla or Subway Surfers release.

          Can I play for free on my phone?

          Yes. Play directly in a compatible mobile or desktop browser with JavaScript and WebGL enabled. No download is required. You can start as a guest; Google sign-in lets you keep your account across devices. Optional coin purchases are available.
